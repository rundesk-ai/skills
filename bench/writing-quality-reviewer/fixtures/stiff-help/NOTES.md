# Notes from the product team for the sharing page

- People share a board so a client or teammate can see it without being invited to the workspace.
- Share menu > Create link. Default is Can view; Can edit is the other option.
- Links expire after 30 days. You can create a new one any time from the same menu.
- Delete a link and anyone holding it loses access right away.
- Viewers don't need an account. Editors do (they're asked to sign in when they open the link).
- Voice guide: talk to the reader as "you", contractions welcome, no hype.
