# Release notes, rebuilt

Something has shifted in how teams keep up with change. This changes everything.

Our new release notes page creates a real sense of momentum. Every release now has a home of its own, with a permanent link you can share.

You can filter notes by product area: Billing, Reports or API. It gives the whole experience room to breathe.

And if you live in a feed reader, there's an RSS feed for release notes.

It's free on every plan.

Nothing about release notes will ever be the same.
