# Two

Jonah saw that the gate to the lower cut had been left open. He felt the wind come up off the water and he heard the heron lift. He noticed a truck parked by the weighbridge, which hadn't been there in the morning.

Ewan was already down there. Ewan is thinking that the boy should never have come back.

Whose is that, Jonah said.

Council, said Ewan. Been coming round. Measuring.

Jonah walked to the edge. Fourteen years since the quarry closed and the water had never been this high. He watched it for a long time, and something in him shifted.
