My notes for the post (my own words):
- stopped using story points 8 months ago on my 5 person team. now we just count tickets per week
- story points turned every planning meeting into a 40 minute argument about whether something was a 3 or a 5
- the counts predict as well as points did. i checked: over 6 months our points-based forecast missed by about 30%, tickets-based missed by about 25%
- the trick is splitting tickets until each is about a day or two of work. thats the actual skill, not the estimating
- doesnt work if your tickets are wildly different sizes. then you need to split first
- point: stop estimating, start splitting
