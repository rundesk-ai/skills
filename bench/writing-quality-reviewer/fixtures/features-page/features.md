# Write the book. Inkwell handles the rest.

## Break it into pieces

Every chapter is its own file. Drag chapters in the sidebar to reorder the book, and nothing else moves.

## Know where you stand

Inkwell counts the words in each chapter and in the whole book as you type, and estimates how long the book takes to read.

## Never lose a good line

Inkwell keeps every version of your book for 90 days. Restore any of them, and the newer versions stay right where they were.

## Just you and the page

Distraction-free mode hides everything but the page you're writing.

## Take it anywhere

Export the whole book or a single chapter as PDF or EPUB.

## Yours, always

It works offline, and your files are plain Markdown on your own disk.
