Inkwell, a desktop writing app for novelists. Confirmed features for the landing page:
- Chapters as separate files, reorder by drag in the sidebar
- Live word count per chapter and for the whole book, plus estimated reading time
- Version history kept 90 days; restore any version without losing newer ones
- Distraction-free mode hides everything but the page
- Export the whole book or one chapter as PDF or EPUB
- Works offline; files are plain Markdown on your disk
