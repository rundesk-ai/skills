# Four

The letter came on the Tuesday. Pell brought it up from the post office with the bread and set it on the table between them and did not say anything, which was not like her.

'Well,' Ana said.

'Well,' Pell said. 'Are you going to open it, or are we going to look at it.'

They looked at it. It was addressed in a hand Ana had not seen in eleven years, and she knew it at once, the way you know a step on the stair.

The rain kept on.
