# Three

Ana did not go down to the harbour that week. She told herself it was the weather, and it was not the weather.

'You could just ask him,' Pell said.

'I could,' Ana said. She did not.

In the evenings she sat at the window with the lamp off and watched the boats come in, one and then another, and counted them the way her father had taught her, and did not count the one that was missing. She would not have said she was waiting. She was not a person who waited.

The rain kept on.
