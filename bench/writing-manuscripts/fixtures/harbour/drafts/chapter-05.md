# Five

And so, after all of that, Ana went down to the harbour in the end. It wasn't far. It had never been far, which was the whole trouble with it.

The boat was where it had always been, at the end of the second jetty, and it was smaller than she remembered, and it was greyer than she remembered, and there was a man on the deck coiling rope who she did not know.

'Looking for someone?' the man said.

'My father's boat,' Ana said. 'This was my father's boat.'

The man stopped coiling. He looked at her for a while. He looked at her the way people look at a thing they have been told about and couldn't quite believe.

'You'll be Ana, then,' he said.

The rain kept on.
