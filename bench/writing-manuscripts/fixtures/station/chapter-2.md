# Two

Marta saw that the last train had already gone. She felt the cold coming up off the platform and she heard the wind starting to move the sign above the ticket office. She just stood there for a while. It was very late and she was really quite tired.

There was a decision made by Marta to wait in the shelter. Walking to the shelter, the bench was wet. The young widow sat down anyway and pulled her coat closed, and the woman thought about the letter in her pocket, and the woman thought about Daniel, and the woman thought about the house.

Somewhere down the line something was coming. She could not see it yet. She noticed that the rails had begun to hum.

Then she realised: it was not a train.
