# Four

Ruth got to the ferry terminal a little after nine, and the woman at the ticket window told her that the last boat had been cancelled because of the fog, which had come in off the water faster than anyone had expected and was now so thick that she could not see the end of the pier from where she stood. Something was wrong.

She sat on one of the plastic chairs by the vending machine and rang her brother, and it rang for a long time before it went to his voicemail, which it never did, because Sam always picked up, even at work, even the time he was in hospital with his leg. She rang again and left a message telling him where she was and asking him to call her back as soon as he got it. Something was wrong.

At half past nine a man in a yellow jacket came in from the pier and spoke quietly to the woman at the window, and then the woman pulled down the shutter and turned off the light in the booth and went out through the back door without looking at Ruth, and the man went with her, and after that it was just Ruth and the hum of the vending machine and the fog pressing up against the glass. Something was wrong.
