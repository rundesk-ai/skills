# One

The river came up in the night. By morning it was in the yard, brown and quiet, carrying a chair from somewhere upstream.

Where'd that come from, Nell said.

Not ours, said her father. He stood in the doorway in his socks and watched it turn slow circles by the shed. Not ours, so not our problem.

She went back to bed. She did not sleep. The water talked under the floor all morning, the way it had in '98, the way her mother said it would again.

Up.
