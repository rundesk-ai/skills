# Three

On the third day the boat came. Nell saw it first, from the landing window, a flat green boat with two men in it and a dog standing at the front. She saw it and then she ran down the stairs to tell her father about the boat.

"There's a boat," she said.

Her father was in the kitchen. He was sitting at the table. He was looking at the water coming under the back door. He did not look up.

I know, he said. I heard them.

Moving on to what happened next, the men tied the boat to the gatepost and waded up the path with the water at their knees. The older one knocks on the open door, which was a strange thing to do, Nell thought, knocking on a door that was already open.

You'll want to come with us, the older one said. It's not stopping.

Her father looked at the boat. He looked at the men. He looked at Nell for a long time.

Out.
