# Four

Priya walks into the workshop and looks around. The lamps are on. Suddenly she sees Dev at the far bench. After that, she walks over to him. Then she looks at what he is building. Next she looks at his hands, which are black with oil, and she looks at the glimmery thing under them, which is a small brass bird with one wing missing.

"Took me a week," Dev says. "Wing's the hard part. Always the wing."

She suddenly realises that the bird is the one from her mother's shop, the one that went missing the winter the shop closed, and that the reason she came here tonight, which was to tell him she was leaving, now seems less important than finding out how he got it.

"Where'd you get it," she says.

"Found it." He doesn't look up. "Market. Tuesday."

Suddenly the lamps flicker.
