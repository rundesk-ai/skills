# Seven

Lena left Harlow at six, before the light. The road was empty. Good.

Her older brother had called at midnight, which meant one of two things, and it was never the good one. Thomas had said only *come*, and then he'd hung up, and she'd sat on the edge of the bed with the phone warm in her hand for 10 minutes before she found her keys.

*He always does this,* she thought. *Always.*

Forty minutes later she was at the coast. The sea was the colour of a knife. She parks behind the bait shop and walks down to the harbour wall, where Tomas is sitting with his legs over the edge and Biscuit pressed against his side.

He looked up when he heard her. Tomas thought she looked tired, older than he remembered.

"You came," he said.

"You said come."

Biscuit thumped her tail twice on the stone. Lena sat down beside them both and didn't say anything for a long while.
