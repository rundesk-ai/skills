# Reference

- **Lena Ortiz**, 34, the point-of-view character. Close third person, past tense, throughout the book.
- **Tomas Ortiz**, her younger brother, 29. Always "Tomas", no h.
- **Biscuit**, Tomas's dog, a grey lurcher.
- **Harlow**, the inland town where Lena lives. The drive from Harlow to the coast takes two hours.
- Numbers are written as words in prose.
- Lena's thoughts are in italics, with no quotation marks.
