# The Umbrella

Gran met me at the the bus stop with her umbrella already open, though it wasn't raining yet. Not a cloud. Just Gran, ready.

"You ain't eaten," she said. "I can tell by your face. Nothin' but bones."

"I ate on the bus."

"Bus food ain't food." She took my bag before I could argue, it's strap already slipping off her shoulder. Their was a smell of cut grass all the way up Mercer Lane, and I let her walk ahead so I wouldn't loose my place behind her, the way I used to when I was seven

At the gate she stopped. Turned. Looked at me a long time.

"Your mother called," she said. "Said you'd recieve a letter. Said I wasn't to ask."

I didn't ask either.
