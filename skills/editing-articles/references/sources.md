## Editing levels and voice
- Copy editing vs line editing: https://prowritingaid.com/copy-editing-vs-line-editing

- Polish the author's voice rather than replace it: https://www.ooliganpress.com/three-tips-on-writing-strong-queries/

## Editing vs ghostwriting, and author queries
- "Editing does not include the addition or removal of any meaning": https://retractionwatch.com/2014/01/21/is-it-ethical-to-ghost-write-a-paper/
- Springer Nature: tracked edits, no significant novel material: https://desk.authorservices.springernature.com/portal/en/kb/articles/do-you-revise-the-content-of-authors-papers
- ACES, author queries (ask, don't fill; offer options; stay humble): https://aceseditors.org/news/2021/author-queries-for-the-nonfiction-editor-how-to-raise-em-and-how-to-phrase-em

- Claim-level fact checking (FActScore), the basis of the trace check: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/
