# Sources

The evidence behind this skill. Read it when a rule here is questioned or needs updating.

## Edit levels
- Editors Canada, Definitions of editorial skills: stylistic editing clarifies meaning, coherence and
  flow, "establishing or maintaining tone, mood, style, and authorial voice"; copy editing checks
  "consistency and continuity of mechanics and facts, including anachronisms, character names, and
  relationships"; proofreading corrects errors in the final material. https://editors.ca/hire/skills
- Editorial Freelancers Association, Editorial services definitions: copyediting corrects spelling,
  grammar, usage and punctuation and prepares the style sheets that keep a manuscript consistent;
  line editing's "main focus is on improving the language and style of the text".
  https://www.the-efa.org/editorial-services-definitions/
- Copy editing vs line editing, and voice most at risk in line editing:
  https://prowritingaid.com/copy-editing-vs-line-editing ·
  https://www.ebookpbook.com/2026/07/04/will-editing-change-your-voice/

## Continuity and the style sheet
- CIEP, Fiction style sheets: characters and their descriptions, places, the timeline, plot threads.
  https://www.ciep.uk/resource/fiction-style-sheets.html
- Penguin Random House, Maintaining continuity (2015): a copy editor tracks "chronology, logistics,
  plot, and personal details", such as breakfast in Philadelphia and Boston before lunch.
  https://authornews.penguinrandomhouse.com/maintaining-continuity-tales-from-the-copy-editor/
- Amy J. Schneider, *The Chicago Guide to Copyediting Fiction*: dialogue, voice, and tracking
  characters, places and events for continuity; builds a fiction style sheet.
  https://www.press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/C/bo183690033.html

## Line-level craft
- TCK Publishing, Filter words: they "filter the scene through a character" and tell the reader to
  look at the character, not through them; keep one where the experience matters more than the
  sensation. https://www.tckpublishing.com/filter-words/
- Emma Darwin, Three things about filtering: filter words are a signal, not bad words; keep one when
  the point of the sentence is a sudden, important realisation.
  https://emmadarwin.substack.com/p/itchy-bitesized-9-three-things-about

## AI tells
- Jeanette Sherman, *Why "Slop" Looks So Sloppy* (essay): model prose is high in cohesion, low in
  coherence and low-variance in cadence; abstract nouns acting as subjects are the most reliable tell;
  stock devices ("not just x, but y", "Still x. Still y.") are what good writers use too, and the
  problem is their reflexive repetition. The basis of "Your own words": lead with the point, people
  as actors, varied rhythm, and no stock glue in the report or in any word the editor adds.
- Wikipedia, Signs of AI writing: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing
